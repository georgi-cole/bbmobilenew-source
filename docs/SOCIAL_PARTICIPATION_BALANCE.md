# Social participation balance

This is separate from the proactive AI alliance/campaign/façade work in PR #1782. It introduces bounded, recoverable consequences for sustained social absence, with the same observation rules for humans and AIs.

## Participation

Daily participation comes from real actions, player-authored incoming replies, explicit alliance decisions and kept commitments. No energy, Influence or money spending is required. One substantive interaction or two distinct smaller interactions count as a meaningful day. Repeated copies of the same small-talk action do not substitute for different interactions. Reading a card, opening the module, passive system chatter, invalidated scenes and automatic expiry do not count as a player reply.

Honest refusals and noncommittal substantive replies count. Public presence is tracked separately: private contact does not become public knowledge. Visible actions and competition wins give the audience something to assess.

## Consequences and recovery

| Mechanic         | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Grace            | No consequences during Days 1–3. No penalty for one or two quiet days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Invitation       | From Day 4, three observed quiet days can prompt an optional, free check-in. At most one every three days, within the existing inbox cap.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Social backing   | From Day 5, sustained absence can erode trust with at most two active observers who have not recently interacted with the contestant. The separate contribution is capped at 6 trust and 3 respect per observer.                                                                                          |
| Trusted floaters | Established trust or loyalty protects a credible quiet bond. Quiet play alone creates no betrayal, suspicion or resentment tag.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Audience         | Public Mode and active Vox can lose at most 4 approval from low visible involvement. Only Charisma changes; Gameplay and Integrity do not. Private-mode approval is unaffected.                                                                                                                           |
| Recovery         | Meaningful contact with an observer or a visible contribution restores their backing adjustment by up to 2 points daily. Visible contributions or competition wins restore up to 2 audience points daily. Recovery only returns previously applied losses; clipped losses create no free recovery credit. |
| Tribunal         | Bounded observer respect changes feed the existing relationship-based evaluation. Competition credit, loyalty, fairness and actual moves remain independent. There is no automatic vote or elimination.                                                                                                   |

The ledger is saved, bounded and idempotent per game day. Reloading or double-advancing does not repeat consequences. Legacy saves start observing actual days rather than inferring an unrecorded history of neglect. Survival and weekend interludes are excluded from assessments. Late Day End or weekend replies still refresh previously observed days on the next assessment. Unresolved votes are not treated as completed days.

This replaces the former runtime quiet-game suspicion path, which interpreted silence as a possible secret agenda. Repeated competition exits retain their existing, separate behavior rule.

The check-in offers a real comparison of positions, a noncommittal conversation or an explanation of a quiet strategy. It never asks the player to buy resources or promise a vote. Ignoring that optional invitation closes it without an additional response penalty.
