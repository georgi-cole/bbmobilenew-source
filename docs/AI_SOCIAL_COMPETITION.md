# AI social competition

AI initiative uses the existing alliance, interaction, relationship and strategic decision systems. Human decisions remain explicit. Survival mode does not use these systems.

## Alliances

- Starting on Day 2, AIs can initiate a three-member group from ordinary positive relationships. A personal pact is not a prerequisite.
- Initiators read their own relationships and witnessed strategic evidence. The shared command processor handles capacity, consent, refusals, boundaries and expiry.
- Leaders can propose recruits up to the existing six-member limit, and propose a co-leader. Members retain their own decisions and may dissent or leave.
- Founding and admission proposals cost the same energy and influence as player proposals. Responses and officer decisions remain free for both.
- At most two founding, admission or officer proposals are initiated across the house each day. Each AI makes at most one approach a day, with a further day to respect refused recruitment or founding terms.
- An alliance with a nominee can recommend an opposing nominee in Classic while still discussing Safety and support. Vox discussions never imply control over an audience eviction vote.

## Campaigns and incoming requests

- Nominees can campaign to eligible voters without belonging to an alliance. Allies can campaign for vulnerable members.
- AIs can pitch a concrete nomination alternative and approach the live Safety holder for themselves or an ally. Safety gets a separate checkpoint before its decision closes.
- All moves use the shared action validation, prices and resolution paths. Choosing a move never guarantees its success.
- Contextual campaigns can follow earlier small talk. Generic interaction-type cooldowns no longer suppress a different decision simply because both use a check-in or deal-offer presentation.
- Background AI approaches have separate daily limits of two routine and three strategic conversations. The existing visible-inbox and per-actor limits remain. Autonomous daily generation is bounded at eight conversations, or ten in Vox.

## Façade nominations

- AI LOHs may attempt one paid, voluntary opening-nomination negotiation per day from Day 2, using their canonical nomination plan.
- An AI invitee weighs its own trust, concern, risk tolerance and perceived support; refusal remains possible. A human gets explicit accept/refuse/no-agreement choices in Incoming.
- Ignoring, dismissing or declining the offer never creates consent. Refusing does not remove the LOH's power to nominate, and no blanket relationship penalty is added for letting this offer expire.
- Accepted arrangements modify the pawn seat while retaining the real target. They neither grant immunity nor control other voters. Consent remains scoped to the current ceremony.
- An accepted arrangement used in nominations records whether the pawn survived or was evicted. An unsafe outcome alone does not prove betrayal.
- These negotiations currently cover the ordinary Classic opening block, including legacy saves with an omitted mode. They do not run during Vox, Survival, Cupid, co-LOH, Double Elimination, public automatic-nominee rules, or incompatible shock windows.

## Proposed inactivity balance — not implemented here

Use recent meaningful participation rather than resource spending: substantive replies, reciprocal support, useful information and kept commitments should count. Quiet competition play should remain viable.

1. Allow an early relationship-building grace period, then offer a clear, situational invitation after a sustained quiet streak.
2. Later, let an isolated contestant lose a bounded amount of social protection, making them a plausible expendable nominee or pawn. Silence must never count as agreeing to that role.
3. Public opinion should react gently to sustained invisible play; visible competition achievements and credible public actions must offset it.
4. Tribunal members should distinguish low social impact from a deliberate floater strategy supported by reliable relationships, timely decisions and kept promises.
5. Permit recovery through a few meaningful interactions. Cap consequences and apply equivalent observations to AIs. Do not award participation points for spending energy or punish one skipped day.

Before tuning numerical penalties, compare matched inactive and engaged runs across multiple competition strengths in Classic and Vox. Existing headless seasons simplify competitions and required choices and have known Final 4 simulation limitations; they are not calibrated human win probabilities.
