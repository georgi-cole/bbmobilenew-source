# Minigame visual direction

The creator's preferred direction is the current Boxpocalypse presentation. Use it as a reference when redesigning or updating other minigames.

- Give each game a polished, cohesive visual identity that belongs in the Big Eye world.
- Use mobile portrait space deliberately, with artwork and game state integrated into the scene.
- Make important events readable through distinct, event-matched animation, sound, and transitions.
- Keep motion paced, smooth, and dramatic enough to follow, with lightweight assets and responsive UI.
- Favor restrained glass and atmospheric surfaces that let the environment show through without hurting legibility.

The creator especially liked Boxpocalypse's presentation and wants future minigame updates to follow this direction.
