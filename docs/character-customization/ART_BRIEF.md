# Character pack art brief

Active revision: 25 October 2026. Execute CS art tickets from [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md). The rejected modular head/body pilot and old four-look proof do not satisfy this brief. Read [ROSTER_PLAN.md](ROSTER_PLAN.md) and [ACCESSORY_PIPELINE.md](ACCESSORY_PIPELINE.md).

## Style and originality

Create original fictional adult cast publicity images matching the game's realistic portraits and softly rendered full figures: believable anatomy, natural skin, plausible hair, controlled studio light, detailed fabric and restrained retouching. Avoid plastic skin, exaggerated eyes, repeated model faces, pasted heads and inconsistent neck/body lighting.

Existing AI characters guide style, crop, scale and scene fit only. Nova, Ash, Bea and all other cast faces/bodies/signature looks must not be copied, renamed, used as identity references or reissued as new players. Generate new identities from written direction. Later edits use only that new identity's accepted own sources.

Review references in public/assets/skins/{Nova,Ash,Bea,Sol,Rune}\_avatar.webp, public/assets/skins/backup-grey-lux/, public/assets/Informal_attires/, public/assets/formal_attires/, and public/assets/tabloid_photos/. Open relevant files before authoring; do not reproduce legacy format mistakes or alpha noise.

## Representation and roster

Target 12 original identities, ten appearing 18–29 and two older adults. The primary player audience is 16–29; all generated characters remain clearly adult. Serve resemblance through curated facial structure, hair texture/length/color, eye color, undertones, age signs and body silhouettes, not anatomy sliders.

Include East/South/Southeast Asian backgrounds, Latine and Afro-Latine representation, a person with albinism, and an androgynous/nonbinary queer fictional character as specified in the roster. Identity/orientation is biography, never a visual diagnosis. Neither selecting a model nor buying an item assigns those facts to the player. Do not rely on costumes or caricatures to signal heritage.

For the albinism character, use an individual natural appearance with pale skin/hair/brows and plausible light eyes. Do not default to glowing red eyes or supernatural styling. People with albinism have varied pigmentation; this is one fictional person's design, not a medical template. See the representation source in RESEARCH.md.

Free characters cover requested identity representation. Paid styling is not the only path to a demographic identity. Outfit and accessory eligibility follows physical fit, not gender. Optional spectrum/pride decoration is usable by everyone.

## Freeze each identity before completing scenes

Retain an identity sheet: character ID, facial structure/landmarks, age target, skin reference, hair silhouette and color, eyes, brows, distinguishing details, build, apparent height and source lineage. Preserve this identity across all six images.

Six candidate identities already exist in look-pilot/masters. Only 01/02 have main portrait candidates. Bodies 03–06 still have unaccepted studio haze/alpha issues. Correct and review their silhouettes; an alpha channel alone does not prove a clean cutout. Do not count these as finished packs.

The existing premium streetwear variants of 01/02 remain future fashion-edition candidates. They are not automatically formal attire. CP-02 presets and MP-00 fragments do not enter the new release by default.

## Body framing and alpha

Use a 2:3 transparent canvas, full hair/hands/shoes and generous side clearance. Existing inspected figures place the crown around 3–5% of canvas height and soles around 95–98%. Example 1024x1536 bounds above alpha 127: Nova casual x308–721/y45–1481, Ash casual x329–733/y82–1473, Rune casual x302–786/y60–1503. Use this as framing guidance, not anatomy measurements.

Keep apparent height and foot baseline consistent between casual/formal. Never stretch a head or body independently to make dimensions fit. No baked floor, studio rectangle, colored haze, stage glow, text, logos or large props. The game already applies scene shadows.

Inspect on light, dark and saturated backgrounds at full resolution and actual scene size. Remove background pixels without making skin/clothes translucent. Check blonde hair, coils, fingers, shoes and garments; reject halos, matte edges, missing parts and detached anatomy.

## Portrait-accessory preparation

Prepare intact transparent portrait-person foregrounds for eligible main/grey/sad states, backgrounds and selective original-hair occlusion masks. Keep the whole person's face/head/neck/shoulders together. This is not permission to revive assembled heads or body/anatomy masks from MP-00.

Use candidate 01 and an eligible second new character such as 03 for the first eyewear proof; candidate 02 already has painted-in glasses. Produce one new original eyewear item and six reviewed fits (two people x three states). The second person's portrait preparation may be partial pack art until its later pack ticket; reuse accepted work in that ticket.

A catalog item needs explicit fit and surface records before sale. Every eyewear design must cover all three promised states for each advertised eligible character. A pack with baked glasses can remain selectable without removable eyewear. State that before purchase. Do not invent a universal fit.

Simple frames/geometric glasses can be authored as precise original SVG; photographic/material artwork uses original raster masters. Follow the image-generation skill for raster creation/edits, retain exact prompts and references, and never repair facial images with improvised pixel generation. See ACCESSORY_PIPELINE for layers, material colors, masks, export and fitting.

## Review in actual scenes

Compare new assets beside existing cast artwork at matched scale. For portraits inspect square/circular 32/48/64/72 px and full preview; eye-color differences may be visible only in larger views. Keep hair and face recognizable at small sizes.

Review casual reveal, grey presentation, Depression Shock, formal Tribunal/finale and After the Eye layouts at 360x780, 390x844 and a larger viewport. The current Tribunal main cutout wrapper uses left -9%, bottom -1%, width 70%, height 100%, contain fit and bottom-center alignment; recheck current styles and responsive overrides.

Use isolated real components when practical. A faithful static mockup must be labeled as such and cannot be reported as runtime/native evidence. Show every mandatory state and the approved accessory on all promised states. Do not imply glasses are present on figures or paparazzi when they are portrait-only.

## Source workflow and deliverables

Freeze returned artwork, source prompts, tool/date/available version metadata, input lineage, SHA-256, art revisions, provenance/rights notes and acceptance record outside public. Temporary tool output paths alone are insufficient. Keep rejected candidates recoverable, clearly marked.

Repeatable export means deterministic resampling/encoding from accepted frozen masters. Image generation itself is nondeterministic. Any changed face, pose, material, source, mask or fit is a new reviewable revision. Keep released revisions immutable.

Art tickets deliver six-image contact sheets, main/grey/sad comparisons, alpha sheets, scene/crop evidence, actual bytes, export settings and observed effort/cleanup. Review unintended marks and commercial-use prerequisites before paid content is admitted.

## Acceptance gates

Separate technical checks from aesthetic acceptance. Record per asset: same-person continuity; distinction from current cast and new siblings; anatomy; skin/hair/eye consistency; natural age/build; lighting; portrait scale; feet baseline; scene crop; alpha; decoding/dimensions/bytes; source retention and reproducible export.

A file passing a validator is a technical result. User visual acceptance is recorded separately and opens dependent art/integration tickets according to BACKLOG. If an essential criterion fails, correct that asset or identify the specific source/artist prerequisite. Do not expand production to mask an unresolved failure.
