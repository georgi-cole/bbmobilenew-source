# Expanded character roster — 27 draft packs

**Art scope:** one authored adult character per pack, each with exactly six distinct game images: casual full figure, main gameplay avatar, grey-lux alternate avatar, formal full figure, sad avatar, and After the Eye paparazzi image. Each pack also derives one store/profile thumbnail from its casual figure. There are no accessory overlays or runtime changes in this art pass.

The original 15-character target was **6 women, 6 men, and 3 androgynous-presenting adults**. With twelve new profiles, the expanded roster is **12 women, 11 men, and 4 androgynous-presenting adults**. These are visual roster directions only; they do not determine a player's identity, pronouns, or story capabilities. All characters read as adults, with the majority designed for the game's 16–29 audience.

## Six image roles and quality rules

- `casual.webp`: transparent full-body casual look.
- `main.webp`: square main-game portrait with restrained gold detail placed behind the character, following Nova's in-game particle distribution. Gold flecks are background nuance and must not cross the face, hair, clothing, or silhouette.
- `grey.webp`: separate square grey-lux portrait using the neutral grey and darker outer tones from `backup-grey-lux/Ali_avatar.webp`.
- `formal.webp`: transparent full-body formal look.
- `sad.webp`: square sad/shocked portrait for the elimination/depression moment.
- `paparazzi.webp`: varied After the Eye scene image.
- `thumbnail.webp`: derived from the casual figure; it is not a seventh game scene.

Full-body PNG masters must carry genuine alpha with clean edges. The export builder records source hashes, dimensions, alpha, and file sizes, and produces a six-role review sheet. Only one selected file per role is kept in each pack. No asset is published to `public/` before visual acceptance.

All six-role r1 drafts through profile 27 are assembled. They have not been visually accepted for shipping; the generated images and review sheets remain art candidates. The earlier glasses experiment and commerce/runtime backlog are out of scope for this roster pass.

## Other coverage opportunities

These are gaps to consider for a later roster expansion, based on the current selectable portraits and AI cast:

- A South Asian man and a wider range of South Asian skin tones, facial features, and hair textures. Profile 17 adds one South Asian woman.
- Indigenous characters, developed with specific communities and references rather than a generic visual shorthand.
- More visibly plus-size body types across women and men; current profiles mostly read as average or slender.
- Characters using mobility aids or other visible disability representation, if supported thoughtfully across gameplay scenes.
- Additional East Asian women with different face shapes, ages, and hairstyles. Mimi already covers the black-hair-and-bangs request, so profile 17 uses a different South Asian identity and wavy curtain fringe rather than copying her.
