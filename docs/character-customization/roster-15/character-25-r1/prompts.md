Use the existing `casual-r1.png` as the single identity and proportion reference. Preserve one adult person's face, skin tone, hair, eyes, age presentation and body shape in every role. Do not generate a replacement casual image or a second person.

3. **Grey-lux alternate:** same identity and casual styling; Ali grey-lux background with darker outer tones.
