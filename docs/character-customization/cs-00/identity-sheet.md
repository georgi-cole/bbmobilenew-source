## Lineage and continuity

The casual and main sources reuse the earlier candidate-01 identity 01 everyday masters. The earlier source prompt states that they were generated as a new fictional adult and used no existing cast image, real-person photo or external character image as an identity reference. The four new masters use only those candidate-01 sources as identity references: casual body for the formal and paparazzi work, and the candidate-01 main portrait for grey and sad.

Nova, Ash, Bea and other existing cast art were consulted only to understand the game's rendering and framing and to compare the finished portraits side-by-side. No existing cast artwork was supplied to image generation. The style comparison is retained in [`evidence/cast-style-comparison.png`](evidence/cast-style-comparison.png); it is not an identity source.

Formal/casual apparent height and feet baseline were aligned through uniform scaling and translation of the complete transparent image. No face, limb, clothing part or alpha region was independently stretched. `report-v3.json` records source and normalized alpha bounds and the exact transforms.
