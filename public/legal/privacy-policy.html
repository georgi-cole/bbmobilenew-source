The Big Eye Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how The Big Eye handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings, player profiles, optional profile images, game progress, season history, and local conversation history on your device. This information is used to save your experience and is not transmitted merely because it is stored locally. You can remove it using available reset controls or by uninstalling the app.

Profile backup and gameplay analytics

Cloud profile backup is an optional adult choice in Player privacy & family. When enabled, profile snapshots are sent to Supabase and include the profile identifier, display name, avatar, selected appearance, achievements, and unlock progress. Photos, biographies, wallet data, season archives, and saved games remain on the device. The backup uses an anonymous account stored by this installation and does not provide restoration on another device. Turning backup off stops future uploads; an existing online copy remains until the profile is deleted or a deletion request is completed.

Optional usage measurement is a separate adult choice in Player privacy & family. When enabled, the app sends pseudonymous gameplay events to Supabase, including guest activity. Saved-profile events include the installation-local profile ID, selected appearance, screen visits and visible time, game modes and numbered days reached, minigame duration, placements and exits, Social module use and action categories, selected gameplay decisions, elimination timing and season placement, marketplace product choices and purchase outcomes, and session duration. Purchase prices are not collected. The profile ID lets the project owner join gameplay events to that profile's backup and display name. Analytics uses a separate anonymous installation identifier. Session events also include broad device type and family, operating system, and browser; an exact device model is included only when the browser provides it. A full browser user-agent string is not sent. Analytics does not include biographies or conversation text. Events carry a build label (QA, production, or development) so test activity can be distinguished. Guest events have no profile ID and are grouped by the anonymous installation identifier. Guest history is not retroactively merged into a newly created profile. Turning measurement off clears unsent events and stops new events. Activity groups describe records, not a verified count of unique people. Deleting a profile on its original installation queues removal of its online backup, contact details, and linked analytics. The app must reconnect to complete the removal. Other analytics rows remain until removed by the project owner or in response to a deletion request. For access or deletion requests, email kolequant@gmail.com.

Optional profile email and game updates

When this feature is enabled, you may add an email while creating or editing a profile. Supabase stores it in a private contact record linked to that profile so the project owner can associate your contact information and communication preference with your player record. The address is unverified and is not used to sign in, recover saves, or automatically combine profiles. It is not copied into local game saves, profile backup snapshots, or gameplay event payloads. Authorized administrators can view it with your preference and broad activity groups, such as returning or inactive player records.

The optional, initially unchecked subscription box gives consent to receive The Big Eye news and playtest invitations by email. Entering an address or accepting this policy does not subscribe you. We record your choice, the displayed consent version, and when the choice changed. Invitations do not automatically enroll you in a testing program. You can turn off this profile's subscription in Edit Profile. Each campaign email must also include an unsubscribe link that stops these messages for the address, without requiring a game login or deleting your profiles. Changing the address requires a new subscription choice.

Your contact address remains linked to the profile until you clear it in Edit Profile or the profile's online record is deleted. Clearing the address also removes its subscription. Deleting a profile requests deletion of its contact record with its backup when the device next connects. Uninstalling alone does not delete online records. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact kolequant@gmail.com.

Optional location

Weather location is optional. If you allow it, the app rounds the device location to a 0.1° area (about 11 km north-south) before sending it through The Big Eye's Cloudflare weather proxy. The proxy requests current conditions from MET Norway and caches the rounded area and response for at least three hours, honoring a later provider expiry and using the provider's last-modified time when refreshing. Cloudflare receives the request's IP address; MET Norway receives the rounded coordinates through Cloudflare rather than your device IP. The proxy does not intentionally log coordinate request bodies. Provider logs and processing are governed by their own privacy terms. The game does not use this data for advertising or tracking. Weather data is from MET Norway under its open-data licence.

Diary Room responses

The release processes Diary Room responses on your device. Diary Room text is not sent to the developer or an AI provider in this release. Do not submit sensitive personal information. If a future version enables online responses, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Purchases and advertising

Optional, one-time digital purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The game reads purchase status from the store to unlock owned features and support purchase restoration. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Supported mobile builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Advertising is optional and requires an adult player's separate choice in Settings → Player privacy & family. The app asks for Google's consent choices before live ad requests and sends non-personalized ad requests. When the SDK runs, Google may collect or share the device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, device identifiers, diagnostic information, user-associated performance data, and information about ads shown. Google describes these data uses for advertising, analytics, performance, and fraud prevention. Non-personalized requests still involve data processing. Advertising can be turned off and Google's privacy choices can be revisited in Settings → Player privacy & family. Test builds can use Google's sample ads. This release does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. No Ads and VIP remove automatic breaks; voluntary rewarded ads remain available by choice. See Google's advertising privacy information.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to deliver an optional feature, comply with law, protect users, or maintain the service.

Children

The current mobile release applies a self-declared minimum age of 18. The Big Eye is not directed to children. Age is not independently verified. Store age ratings and target-audience settings reflect the final game content and local requirements.

Security, retention, and choices

Network services use HTTPS. You may deny or revoke location permission. Local game data remains until reset or uninstall. For privacy or deletion requests, email kolequant@gmail.com or visit the official support page.

Changes

We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest version.
