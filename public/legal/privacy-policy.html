The Big Eye Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how The Big Eye handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings, player profiles, optional profile images, game progress, season history, and local conversation history on your device. This information is used to save your experience and is not transmitted merely because it is stored locally. You can remove it using available reset controls or by uninstalling the app.

Optional location

Location starts off and is unavailable for teen or unknown-age players. An adult must enable weather location in Player privacy & family and grant device permission before coordinates can be requested.

If you grant location access, the app may send coordinates to Open-Meteo to request current weather and select a matching ambient visual theme. Location is optional, is not required to play, and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not intentionally retain coordinates after the request.

Diary Room responses

The release processes Diary Room responses on your device. Diary Room text is not sent to the developer or an AI provider in this release. Do not submit sensitive personal information. If a future version enables online responses, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Purchases and advertising

Optional, one-time digital purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The game reads purchase status from the store to unlock owned features and support purchase restoration. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Supported mobile builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Advertising is optional and requires an adult player's separate choice in Player privacy & family. When enabled, Google may receive device information and identifiers, IP addresses, diagnostic information, and ad interactions to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. The app requests non-personalized ads and asks for Google's required privacy choices before requesting live ads. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing. You can turn advertising off and revisit Advertising privacy choices in Player privacy & family. Test builds can use Google's sample ads. No Ads and VIP remove automatic breaks; voluntary rewarded ads remain available by choice. See Google's advertising privacy information.

Sharing and sale

Optional usage measurement starts off and is unavailable for minors. If an adult enables it and a measurement service is configured, screen and game events with a session identifier may be sent to that service. Profile text and photos are not part of these events. Switching it off stops new events and removes the local event buffer. The game can retrieve static content and configuration after admission; hosting providers receive ordinary network metadata.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to deliver an optional feature, comply with law, protect users, or maintain the service.

Children

This version defaults to a minimum player age of 18 while its content rating is reviewed. This is a product access policy, not an assigned store rating. The teen permission path described below applies only to a reviewed version whose minimum age permits it. Parent permission cannot override a minimum player age or applicable content restriction.

The Big Eye is not directed to children under 13. Store age ratings and target-audience settings reflect the final game content and local requirements.

A neutral age question stores an age group and permission choices on this device. This is self-declaration, not identity verification. Play for players declaring an age under 13 remains locked because a verified parent-consent service is not connected. The parent screen sends no consent request or parent/child personal information. A higher store or local legal age restriction still applies.

Players aged 13–17 are asked to involve a parent or guardian in permission for local play. This acknowledgment is not verified parental consent for online processing. Optional location, usage measurement, online Diary Room/result services, advertising, purchases, and restore requests are unavailable for teen players. Adults can choose optional features separately and withdraw choices in Settings → Player privacy & family. Enabling a setting does not activate an unavailable service.

Stored or newly entered younger profile ages can tighten restrictions. Switching profiles or guest mode does not lift them. Profile deletion is available even on the locked screen under Privacy, saved data, and help. Parent help and genuine age corrections can be discussed with support; do not send a child’s personal information with an initial inquiry. Age corrections cannot currently unlock play through this app.

Security, retention, and choices

Network services use HTTPS. You may deny or revoke location permission. Local game data remains until reset or uninstall. For privacy or deletion requests, email kolequant@gmail.com or visit the official support page.

Changes

We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest version.
